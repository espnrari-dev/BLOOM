ORGANIC BLOOM PESTICIDE - NEEM + GARLIC
- 1L warm water
- 1 tsp cold-pressed neem oil
- 1/2 tsp castile soap (dr bronners)
- 1 clove garlic crushed, 1 pinch cayenne
- Shake, let sit 12h, strain

Spray: mist leaves + soil at sunset, not midday
Test 1 leaf first, 24h check
Re-spray every 5-7 days, after rain
Keeps aphids, mites, whitefly off - safe for bees if sprayed at dusk
